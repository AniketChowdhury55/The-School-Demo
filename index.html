Academics & Programs

A well-rounded curriculum designed to challenge, inspire, and prepare students at every stage of their academic journey.

Pre-Primary

A play-based, Montessori-inspired foundation that sparks curiosity and develops fine motor skills in a safe, nurturing environment for ages 3–5.

Primary School

Building strong literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking through an engaging, activity-led curriculum aligned with national standards (Grades 1–5).

High School

Rigorous academics paired with career counseling and competitive exam preparation, empowering students to achieve their university goals (Grades 6–12).

STEM & Innovation

Hands-on robotics, coding labs, and science fairs cultivate the innovators and problem-solvers of tomorrow across all grade levels.

About Our School

Welcome to Patna Heritage Academy. For over two decades, we have been committed to building an institution where students discover their potential and develop into responsible, compassionate, and forward-thinking citizens.

Our mission is simple: to provide every child with a world-class education that balances academic rigour with creativity, sports, and character building. We believe that the best education happens when students feel safe, valued, and inspired.
